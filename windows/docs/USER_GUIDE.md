# G OPTICALS: User Guide

For shop staff and the shop owner using G OPTICALS every day. Which menus you see depends on your role and on the modules in your licence.

## 1. Signing in

1. Start **G OPTICALS** from the desktop or Start Menu shortcut.
2. Enter the user name and password your administrator gave you.
3. If two-factor sign-in is on for your account, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

After several wrong passwords the account is locked for a few minutes. Wait, then try again. If you have forgotten your password, ask an administrator to reset it.

The app signs you out after a period of inactivity. Always use **Sign out** when you leave a shared PC.

## 2. First steps for the owner (once)

| Where | What to set |
|---|---|
| **Settings → Store & Clinic Profile** | Your shop's legal name, address, phone, e-mail, tax registration number, logo and clinic registration number. **These print on every bill, receipt, prescription and report.** |
| **Settings → Business & Localization** | Country, currency, tax name (GST / VAT / Sales Tax) and whether tax is charged, document numbering, negative-stock rule |
| **Settings → Bill & Invoice Designer** | The layout of your printed bills |
| **Settings → Bank Master** | Your bank accounts and UPI, for Bank and UPI payments |
| **Administration → Users** | One user per staff member, with a role (Sales Executive, Cashier, Optometrist, Accountant, …) |
| **Administration → Doctors Master / Service Master** | Optometrists and doctors; billable services such as fitting or eye tests |
| **Inventory → Opening Stock** | The stock you already have, typed in or imported from Excel |

## 3. Daily work

| Task | Menu |
|---|---|
| Eye test and prescription | **Optical Services → Eye Test**. Each visit is saved as its own record with its own Test No. |
| Book or confirm an appointment | **Optical Services → Appointment**. Confirmations can be sent by WhatsApp. |
| Retail sale (frame, lens, services) | **Sales → New Sale**. Search the patient or customer, add items, take payment (Cash / Card / UPI / Bank, split if needed), then print. |
| Quick counter billing | **Sales → POS Billing** |
| Orders, quotations and deliveries | **Sales → Orders**. An order becomes an invoice when it is delivered and settled. |
| Collect a balance | **Sales → Payments** |
| Return | **Sales → Sales Return** / **Purchase → Purchase Return** |
| Wholesale (dealers) | **Wholesale Distribution** |
| Receive stock from a supplier | **Purchase → Purchase Entry**, then **Barcode Printing** for labels |
| See stock | **Inventory → Stock Overview** |
| Cash and bank | **Financial → Cashbook / Bank Book / Day Book**, or **Accounts** for vouchers and expenses |
| Reports | **Reports** (sales, purchase, stock, tax, profit, eye tests) |

Busy screens such as New Sale, POS Billing, Eye Test and Purchase Entry have keyboard shortcuts. Where a screen has them, **Ctrl + /** shows the list.

## 3a. Interface size

The desktop app opens at **100 %** interface size. To make the screens smaller (more rows and columns fit) or change it back on this PC:

- Click the **Interface size** button in the header (its tooltip shows the current size, for example "Interface size: 100%") and choose **75 %, 80 %, 90 % or 100 %**, or
- Use the **View → Interface size** menu, or **Ctrl + Plus** / **Ctrl + Minus**.

**Reset to default (100 %)** (or **Ctrl + 0**) restores the default. The choice is saved for your Windows user on this PC and stays after a restart. It changes only how big the screens look. Printed bills, receipts, labels and PDFs always keep their real paper size, and your Windows display settings are not changed. To fit more on a small screen, choose 90 %, 80 % or 75 %.

## 4. Printing and sharing

- Bills, receipts and prescriptions print with **your shop's** name, logo and address from the Store & Clinic Profile.
- **Print** opens the app's **print preview**. It shows the real pages exactly as they will print: paper size, margins, logo, tables and customer details. In the preview you choose:
  - **Printer.** Your paper printers come first, with the Windows default marked. **Save as PDF** saves exactly these pages as a PDF file. Virtual printers such as Microsoft Print to PDF or OneNote are listed separately, because they make files, not paper.
  - **Paper size.** *As designed* uses the size set in Settings → Bill & Invoice Designer: A4, A5, or an 80 mm receipt roll cut to the length of the receipt. Choosing A4, A5 or Letter fits the page onto that sheet instead.
  - **Margins**, **Copies**, and **Orientation**. Orientation can only be changed for screens and reports that have no fixed page size.
- The preview remembers the printer you last used for each kind of document, so bills, receipts and labels can each go to their own printer.
- If no paper printer is set up on the PC, the preview says so and offers **Save as PDF**. To print on paper, add the printer in Windows **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**, then press **↻** in the preview.
- **Windows print dialog…** in the preview opens the Windows dialog for printer-specific options such as the paper tray or two-sided printing. That dialog shows "This app doesn't support print preview". This is expected: the app's own window is the preview.
- Receipts and labels are sent to the printer at their exact size. If a receipt or label printer feeds or cuts wrongly, check the paper or label size in that printer's Windows driver.
- Shortcuts in the preview: **Ctrl + P** prints, **Ctrl + S** saves a PDF, **Esc** closes.
- **Share on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the bill summary, signed with your shop name and phone number. Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.

## 5. Branches and companies

- If your business has several branches, the branch you are working in is shown in the header. Stock and bills belong to that branch.
- If you are a member of more than one company, switch with the company selector in the header. Each company's data is separate.

## 6. If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Server is not available" | The server PC may be off, or the network is down. Tell your administrator. |
| "Being updated or restored" | Wait a few minutes. An update or restore is running. |
| "Read-only" / licence message | Tell the owner. The licence needs renewing. You can still view and print. |
| A message at the bottom of the screen after saving | Read it. It says what was saved or what is missing. |

Your administrator has the **Troubleshooting Guide** for everything else.
